# Correcting media history

Open a media item's activity history. Use a record's menu to edit or delete it.

- Correcting progress to 100% can complete the item. Reducing it can remove an automatic completion.
- Correcting or deleting an episode watch also updates the automatic show or podcast completion.
- A completion you logged yourself stays unless you edit or delete it.
- Editing an automatic completion makes it your explicit record. Later automatic updates leave it alone.
- Deleting an automatic completion keeps its supporting activity but prevents that same occurrence
  from being recreated. A later rewatch or reread can still complete again.

Corrections do not resend notifications or playback pushes. They do not remove library membership
or change monitoring. Provider updates do not erase past completion records; new episodes can still
change current coverage.

Corrections and their derived figures apply together when you save. If the server cannot finish the
correction, the save fails and your previous history remains; retry the save.
