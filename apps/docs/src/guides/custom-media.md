# Edit Custom Media

You can edit media, people, companies, and groups that you created yourself. Provider metadata
cannot be changed through this editor.

1. Open the item's details.
2. Select **Edit**.
3. Change its name, properties, images, or available relationships, such as credited people and
   companies.
4. Select **Save** to return to its details. Select **Cancel** to leave without saving.

Fields and images you do not change are kept. If the item or its relationships changed elsewhere,
the save stops. Reopen the editor to load the latest data before trying again.

For media, a full publish date supplies the publish year when the year is empty. An existing year
is kept when you change the date. Change or clear the year yourself if it must follow the new date.

Credits are edited on the media item or credit-bearing group, not on the person or company. Group
members are edited on the group.

## Seasons and episodes

Custom shows and podcasts have their own seasons and episodes. On the **Episodes** tab, select
**Add season** or **Add episode**. Season and episode numbers start at the next number; you can
change them before saving. A season number cannot change after the season is created, and a season
or episode stays with the show, season, or podcast it was created under.

Open an episode and select **Edit episode**, or select **Edit season** above a season's episodes.
**Delete** removes a season or episode only when it has no history and, for a season, no episodes.
Delete its history or episodes first. Provider seasons and episodes cannot be edited or deleted.

Duplication is not available here.
