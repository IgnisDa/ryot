# IMDb

Import your IMDb watchlist or ratings as a CSV file. Watchlist entries are marked as planned.
Entries with a rating or rated date are marked as watched. Ratings are converted to a 100-point scale.

1. Open your [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com) watchlist or ratings list.
2. Select **Export this list** at the bottom.
3. Upload the CSV file under **Settings > Import data**.
